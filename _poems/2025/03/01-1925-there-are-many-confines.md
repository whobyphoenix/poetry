text: |
   There are many confines, dungeons, and wards,
   This is one of the worst.
   Enclosed in a nutshell,
   No mirrors to face,
   A queen of infinite space,
   Dreaming at the center of the maze.
   A dream itself is but a shadow
   Of the roads yet to walk.
   All your betters, all my heroes
   Found shelter in a cloak.
   In action how like angels,
   In apprehension how like gods,
   Outstretching the rainbows,
   Punishing the river
   That wouldn't let them cross.
   I have an eye of you,
   You have all of me -
   Let them be.
