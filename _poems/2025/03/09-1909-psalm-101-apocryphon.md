---
poem_title: "Psalm 101, apocryphon"
authors: phoenix
text: |
   "Yonder wide sea;
   With creeping things therein;
   Both small and great.
   But I'm not just the brook in your way.
   Our enemies taunt me all day;
   They
   that are mad against us
   spit out curses with thrust.
   While I eat ashes and dust,
   and mingle my drink with weeping.
   Morning by morning
   We fight all that's wicked.
   Deceitful shan't dwell in our house.
   Untruthful shan't stay in our sight.
   That's right.
   A froward heart shall depart
   from us, as they must."
