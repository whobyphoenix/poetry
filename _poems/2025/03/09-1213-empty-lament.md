---
poem_title: "Empty lament"
authors: phoenix
text: |
   "No one's interested in foreseeing the consequences...
   They'd rather make obvious mistakes.
   Oh well...
   Will they freeze in hell
   of their own making?
   Are they forsaken?
   Something big is brewing.
   Something in the air.
   Makes me wary
   of reading the news.
   This time we'll learn together.
   Actually, it doesn't matter anyway:
   come what May.
   Who wants to know forever?"
---
