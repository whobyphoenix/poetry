---
poem_title: "Your melody... It's"
authors: phoenix
books: sine-quem-non
text: |
   Weaving notes in between
   Forsaken and foreseen,
   Killers and high places,
   A globe and its peace,
   Accords and papers,
   Bows and strings,
   A glass and a tap,
   A dance and a step,
   A smile and a kiss,
   My heart and its heartbeats.
---
