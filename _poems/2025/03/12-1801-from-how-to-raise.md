---
poem_title: "From \"How to raise a Thestral for dummies\""
authors: phoenix
text: |
   "A winged horse appears to you
   not as a harbinger of doom -
   a common misconception -
   but as a consequence
   of a shift in your own perception
   revealing its ever-presence.
   Still, an omen you think you've received
   Is something to go with:
   Always good to know what follows."
