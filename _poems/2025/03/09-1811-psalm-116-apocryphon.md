---
poem_title: "Psalm 116, apocryphon"
authors: phoenix
text: |
   "The chorus of liars
      misled.
   The cords of the past
      compassed.
   The straits of the nether-world
      got hold.
   I found trouble and sorrow.
   Although,
   I trusted *you* even when I spoke:
   'I'm greatly ill.'
   I said in my haste:
   'All men are liars.'
   Now I lift up the cup of desire
   and offer you all what's left 
   in me like daily bread."
