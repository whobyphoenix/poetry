---
poem_title: "Venus"
authors: phoenix
images: 11-1028-1.jpg
text: |
   Acidic clouds whirl around;
   hurricane-force winds
   strike like corrosive whips.
   Underneath
   lies extreme heat.
   Pressure is so crushing
   that upon touching her skin
   even lead would melt.
   Hotter than anything,
   this hellscape of a planet
   may have once been habitable.

   (Thank you, @NASA)
