---
poem_title: "Like an honest man and a soldier"
authors: phoenix
text: |
   Is there GetAGripAI,
   trained to speak plainly
   and to the point,
   and very opinionated preferably?
   Like you tell it your huge personal issues
   and it goes "hey, man, that's bullshit;
   you create your own problems
   and then keep drowning in self-pity;
   just stop already:
   go help a stranger
   or at least feed a pigeon, 
   you'll feel tonnes better;
   and do get some perspective:
   children are dying right now
   of war and hunger...
   there goes another one, and another...
   maybe you should do something about that, huh?.."
   (Not to be used all the time,
   only once in a while...
   And never by obsessive self-blamers.
   I mean those with savior complex.
   Conscientious losers must be rooted out and forbidden the service!
   No, seriously, for pity's sake don't serve these... idealists...
   They may die of self-loathing...)
