text: |
   The courage of body conquers one,
   The courage of mind conquers many.
   And when you stay true
   To the kindness in you,
   Impassable roads
   Lead you right over
   Uncrossable streams,
   Unclimbable mountains,
   And all that spiders in between.
   Straight through the danger zone,
   Be it Gobi desert or the Amazon.
