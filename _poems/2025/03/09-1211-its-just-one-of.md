text: |
   "It's just one of those days,
   when there's nothing but rimes in every phrase,
   every structure of rhythm, every voice...
   It seems, I'm unable to pause.
   Keep tugging on threads,
   pulling lines from thin air, rabbits from hats.
   It all started with you and Cohen.
   I'm still not sure if results are poems.
   But it doesn't matter anyway.
   It happens like breathing nowadays.
   An in-breath of what's beautiful in an interesting way,
   and out it flows.
   It only stops
   when I'm rag-dress-rehearsed.
   And even then, sometimes it goes
   only the mirror is reversed,
   turned to face what's within;
   and it's definitely neither beautiful nor interesting.
   I don't know how to explain this fountaining.
   A way of coping?
   A swan-song?
   Must be this damned liminality, that's all."
