---
poem_title: "Someone,"
authors: phoenix
images: 23-1148-1.jpg
text: |
   with a brain in their head,
   who, without going mad,
   continues over and over for years
   to devote all their being,
   all the force of their thought
   to the noble endeavor
   of cornering their goals
   while dragging ideals
   along on slippery slopes.
---
