text: |
   A fountain is this perfect way
   to experience time at different paces,
   depending on what you concentrate.
   There's longevity of overall patterns
   and randomness
   of water-drops trajectories,
   and externalities
   like sudden wind,
   or fallen leaves,
   or playing children,
   or shadows of birds
   flying above,
   bringing in uncertainties.
   And all of it sings a hymn to gravity.
   And when it finally stops
   this is the time for birds
   to get in to quench their thirst,
   while ripples wobble and subside.
   Meanwhile,
   the little lights,
   the Banner of Peace lookalikes,
   are blinking,
   semaphoring,
   reminding
   that the water will surely flow
   again.
