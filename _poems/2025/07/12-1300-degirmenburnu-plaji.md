text: |
   Strange things happen to me sometimes…
   Remember that bracelet I bought
   To remind me to believe and smile?
   Так вот.
   I liked it at lot,
   except that one section
   that looked like a chain link.
   And guess what?
   Just now it kind of came off
   all by itself, broke
   in exactly the right spot.
   Now I have the same bracelet,
   albeit a bit shortened,
   but much more unique
   and without the chained bit.
   And I like it even more.
   I love you, by the way, have I said it?
