---
poem_title: "Yildiz, Beşiktaş"
authors: phoenix
text: |
   Rumi is full of contradictions, too,
   because the truth
   is so elusive.
   There he says we are in court
   at every conscious moment,
   and only at night
   can find a blissful respite
   from justice and judgement.
   (With the exception of a mystic
   who can sleep through it all,
   like a pen in the hand of the Lord.)
   And there he points out
   how privation and defect
   are mirrors of the beauty of reality,
   noting how
   a master can only try his hand
   at what's imperfect yet.
   If defects are precious,
   why is Divine justice so harsh?
   Why not accept
   the truth of a trend?
   Why free us only at night?
