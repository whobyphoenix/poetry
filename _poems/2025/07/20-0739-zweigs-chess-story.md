---
poem_title: "Zweig's Chess Story"
authors: phoenix
images: 20-0739-1.jpg
text: |
   Telegraph boys in cocked caps hustle,
   dash through the last-minute bustle,
   inquisitive children sway
   on the companionways,
   while the orchestra and I play
   a game of who's more imperturbable.
   We stand apart from this hubbub
   and flaring flashbulbs,
   and the rare bird
   we've got on board
   with a meteoric career and total apathy,
   the lumpish, taciturn, awkward,
   and broad-browed prodigy.
