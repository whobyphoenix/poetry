text: |
   Conversing with the dead
   has this advantage that
   they cannot betray,
   or push away,
   or make you fall
   on your own sword
   with a single word,
   or reject a gift
   however imperfect.
   Rejection isn't in their books
   quite literally,
   for every literary line
   is an invitation
   into the divide
   mystery of the mastery.
   Same is true for melodies.
   There's only "yes"
   in every masterpiece,
   grand or small,
   a street graffiti
   or a prophetic mural.
   Art is always welcoming:
   refusing a refusal,
   rejecting a rejection,
   extending the invitation
   to everyone.
   Dirty deeds
   are better done
   with dirty instruments.
   The rider will hold me soon,
   I have no doubt.
   The only question is
   whether we'll be allowed
   to kiss,
   as I've been promised.
