---
poem_title: "Mithat Paşa Cd."
authors: phoenix
text: |
   A skinny boy, dragging
   a carriage bigger than him,
   is honked at by a taxi:
   he stopped midroad for a breathing -
   the road is uphill.
   To start moving,
   he jumps,
   grabs handles of the carriage,
   spots me looking at him.
   He's visibly displeased:
   with the looking part,
   the undue curiosity -
   the rest is routine.
---
