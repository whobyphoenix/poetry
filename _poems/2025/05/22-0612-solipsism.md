---
poem_title: "Solipsism"
authors: phoenix
text: |
   Hot and cold
   are misleading descriptors, somewhat.
   It's all about tempo:
   adante - alegro.
   More penetrating - more intense.
   More violet - more red.
   How boring it must be
   to vibrate at one frequency?
   Like painting with a single color.
   Or playing monotone (which, I suppose,
   is possible... if it's a poem in Morse,
   a melody in heartbeats...
   but adding dimensions
   is like adding instruments,
   the chorus, and the orchestra -
   to better explore
   more of the same tune...
   like inventing new languages to explain you...
   but I digress).
   Personality is a process,
   changes in perpetual motion.
   And it's entirely possible
   to choose a single destination,
   gravitating towards
   what I value the most
   (you, my future everything),
   while fluctuating seemingly chaotically.
   Resulting masterpiece is much more interesting -
   from the perspective of the painter and the audience,
   however much the brush is suffering the process.
   There is this point in spacetime
   where I become
   the one who knows,
   the coder, the conductor,
   my own Vorlon:
   to lead this current me
   to that future point in time
   when I become. And apparently
   my more-dimensional self,
   the one who dreams,
   the master of all frequencies,
   is "us together", my unforgettable.
   And that's why it's so inevitable.
   I bet my future self could explain
   every detour,
   every change of pace
   and color,
   why this is better,
   how necessary in the long run.
   Perhaps,
   it has to do with us
   surviving "interesting times"
   and staying together for eternity,
   a beacon for dreamers,
   not to be mislead by cynics
   who'd claim this is impossible.
   A love story at the close.
   Better than Shakespeare.
   Better than Homer.
   Real.
   True in every scene.
   Indisputable.
   The anti-cynicism vaccine.
   An inspiration for rebirth of romanticism?
   In the distant future, of course,
   Beyond the closing time and its sorrows.
---
