text: |
   What am I to say
   After the first week of May?
   Am I OK?
   I'm barely moving, I am running away.
   Only it's the other way around.
   Kind of.
   Away - in my mind - from staring eyes in the skies behind every cloud.
   Beyond its blue gaze I see my own face:
   Distorted rippling image, sometimes ablaze.
   I'm also like a rock: a flaming fluid, perpetually stable super-solid - revolving around a single thought,
   I hope I am running toward.
   Away is no my way.
   So maybe I'm better than OK.
   I am myself in a way.
