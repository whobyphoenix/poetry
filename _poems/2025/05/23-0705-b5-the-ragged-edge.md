---
poem_title: "B5: The Ragged Edge"
authors: phoenix
text: |
   Citizen G'Kar... Who, pray tell, just leaves their writings
   lying around in a privacy of their cabin
   under a lock and key
   where anyone could simply break in
   and "liberate" your manuscript
   of observations,
   unscrupulous laments,
   inner thoughts,
   intimate or not?
   So it's all on you, you poor "prophet"...
   Haven't you heard?
   "Never create anything, it will be misinterpreted,
   it will chain you and follow you for the rest of your life."
   Maybe that's why
   wise prophets usually decline
   the authorship and claim
   their hand was guided...
   As to the Guide...
---
