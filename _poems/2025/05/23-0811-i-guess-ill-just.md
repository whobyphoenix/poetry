---
poem_title: "I guess I'll just pop the question."
authors: phoenix
text: |
   We've never established how touchy you are...
   Physically - not at all so far.
   How about with humor?..
   Are you a prideful wind-catcher
   Or not as such?..
   How much
   Fun of you can I make trying to make you laugh?..
   Of course, only in private.
   Gently
   And very lovingly,
   Rest assured.
   And you, a master of words
   And a native speaker, of course
   Would win every time.
   Care to guess what'd be your prize?..
   And sweetie, do try to lose once in a while.
   This is my earnest advice.
   If only to learn what follows.
   Aren't you curious?..
   Aren't you mine?
