---
poem_title: "Agni-yoga"
authors: phoenix
text: |
   One *can* stay in the Now,
   I get the appeal:
   dissolving time in eternity
   of a sound...
   But it's not *the only*
   "better way to be".
   One can also observe the melody
   as a painting,
   all brush strokes at once.
   Introduce the meaning by observing patterns.
   The mystery is brought about by magicians
   who dare to become creators.
---
