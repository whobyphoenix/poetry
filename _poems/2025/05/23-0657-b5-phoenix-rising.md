poem_title: "B5: Phoenix rising"
authors: phoenix
text: |
   What's there to understand, mr. Bester?
   Tribalism is wrong.
   In any shape or form.
   People are people.
   The real question is
   what kind of a person
   would mistreat someone
   just because of their origins
   or any other feature that isn't chosen?
   The Golden Rule clearly tells you *not*
   to make this distinction.
   What kind of a person would violate this?
   And yes,
   mr. Bester, Byron is right
   to prefer suicide
   over going back to your "family".
   However much you'd like for him to convert,
   to return like a "prodigal son" to your tribe,
   He objects.
   To your methods.
   You see?
   And this makes you *not* his kin.
   Pity,
   that this was
   the only noble choice
   left to him.
   And even more so,
   that, since violence breeds violence,
   phoenix thus arising
   will bring more
   death and war.
   The war that Byron most certainly didn't want.
   And his solution to the bigger issue is also right:
   when people can't
   live together then the better way is to separate -
   there's plenty of space;
   separation is not ideal but it's better
   than extermination
   or repression and domination.
   Live and let live - at a distance -
   if for some reason it isn't possible to coexist
   in Peace.
commentary: |
