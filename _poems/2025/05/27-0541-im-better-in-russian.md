   I'm better in Russian, am I not?
   In English I'm just playing with words.
   Whatever comes to mind goes.
   You come to mind the most, so...
   I stopped my bicycle to write down
   completely different lines
   which I don't even remember anymore.
   It all just happens, no intent at all:
   reflection of a reflection of the world.
   I consider it a positive reinforcement
   from whoever's watching over.
   I must've done well today:
   said (in writing! under my own name!)
   things I've never said before.
   I deserve a treat.
   (You deserve it permanently.)
