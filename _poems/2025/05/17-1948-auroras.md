---
poem_title: "Auroras"
authors: phoenix
text: |
   Solar wind
   interacting with magnetic field.
   Oxygen. Nitrogen.
   A waltz of colors.
   Southern tribes see glow of torches,
   spiritual campfires.
   Imho, Northern myths
   are more poetic.
   Especially that of Inuits:
   dead playing football
   with a walrus skull.
   Who'd think of this, huh?
   Or, Sami's one:
   a sky fox sweeping snow with its tail,
   thus the "fox flames".
   Apparently, Чуваши
   see a distributor of souls
   to newborns.
   And all of course
   would read a "message in the sky",
   an omen, or a sign.
   False pattern-matching is so strong in us...
   It's only prudent to stay cautious.
---
