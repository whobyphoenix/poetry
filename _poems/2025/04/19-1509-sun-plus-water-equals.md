---
poem_title: "🫠❤️🫵🎉🎂"
authors: phoenix
text: |
   Sun plus water equals rainbows;
   But also
   Sunlight turns
   Any old lake
   Into a shimmering mess,
   Reflected fireworks,
   Something like Christmas,
   A blinking delight,
   Impossible fire hazard;
   Makes you squint 🥰
   No matter the shades 😎
   Like when you smile,
   My smile of sunshine ✨️
---
