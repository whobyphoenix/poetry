text: |
   All of it cuts too deep:
   one doesn't have an armor,
   or a thick skin,
   or any kind of cover.
   Am I re-learning how to feel?
   The wind is too tender here - burns my skin.
   Thank heavens for music, and especially for guitarists,
   even if they are rubbish, it's still a sin
   to not enjoy; at least a little.
   I'm not keen on making friends;
   it takes a lot to lower defenses.
   Yes, it's because of that betrayal.
   I forgave her, but only recently.
   She handled it poorly,
   but I understand. Actually,
   what hurt me the most was hate:
   truly hating her like that.
   Him I didn't even blame;
   he tried to be honest,
   and I appreciated that,
   even back then. He's only a man.
   And by then I already knew he wasn't the one,
   I just couldn't admit it,
   that I could've made
   such a mistake; only it felt
   a bit like family. A painful loss.
   But I'd been pushing him away
   for about a year by then. It was my doing.
   Should've been more honest with myself.
   I'd dumped him a year before,
   over a stupid pretence of a reason,
   without even realizing what it really was:
   that he wasn't the right man.
   Возвращаться - плохая примета;
   особенно к бывшим.
   Or maybe I didn't appreciate him,
   didn't make enough effort.
   People change; two people
   have to change together to stay together.
   And I didn't pay enough attention.
   I know all these mistakes; this isn't a recent revelation.
   I just never thought I'd have another chance
   not to make them.
   I know exactly how things should go
   for this to last forever.
