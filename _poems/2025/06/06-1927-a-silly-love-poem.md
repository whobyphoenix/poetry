---
poem_title: "A silly love poem"
authors: phoenix
text: |
   I love you more than chocolate,
   more than ice cream.
   I love you more than my cat,
   and that's saying something.
   Basically,
   it's more than anyone or anything.
   Yes, even more than music and sci-fi,
   and walking in the rain at night time.
   I wonder if it'll get harder with years
   to invent new ways of telling you this.
   I think not.
   There are always new languages,
   and this is a great creative outlet.
---
