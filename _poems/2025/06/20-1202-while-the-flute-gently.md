---
poem_title: "While the flute gently weeps; improving upon Rumi"
authors: phoenix
text: |
   Weeping flute and drumming beats
   raise the goof from its knees
   to pull once more
   at the reasoning rope,
   while the white dove
   is thrashing around
   in the heart's cage,
   wobbling with fear and rage
   unable to get out.
   The bed of the river is so ancient
   and yet
   the banks are green once again
   comes the flood.
   Upon hearing the drum
   the aspiring falcon
   takes off
   seeking in circles
   the wrist of its king,
   homing in.
---
