---
poem_title: "Know"
authors: phoenix
text: |
   A throw of a stone is never alone.
   Can you postpone
      the sunrise?
   A night, a life, a shimmering light,
   I'd give it to you in a blink of an eye:
   holding back seems so criminal somehow.
   I won't. And anyhow,
   can you temper the sunrise?
   See,
      down below is this brilliant net.
   Captivating…
   More than that:
   enchanting!
   A seagull hovers in front of my eyes,
   a frozen remnant in amber,
   freezing this moment in time.
   Now,
      can you hold back the sunrise?
