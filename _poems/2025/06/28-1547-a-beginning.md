---
poem_title: "A Beginning"
authors: phoenix
text: |
   "It won't be long" sing all the spirits:
   the cats, the trees, the seagulls.
   Of course, I do believe them,
   what else is there?
   The essence of my existence
   is to begin at the beginning
   of every quant of being:
   the Now of a Phoenix.
   Sometimes the Time
   drags in the minutes.
   But just between us,
   he's very much an impotent.
   And maybe that's the reason
   why he's so angry with me.
   For I am
   at the other end of the spectrum,
   a living embodiment
   of what's the opposite.
   He better just admit defeat
   and not oppose me.
   His nervous ticks are just ridiculous.
   Maybe if I provoke
   him a little more
   with my unscrupulous jokes,
   he'll break apart completely,
   lose all the gears,
   and simply let us be -
   happy?..
   which is to be together,
   in timeless bliss, forever.
---
