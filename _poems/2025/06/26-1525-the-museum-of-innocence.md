---
poem_title: "The Museum of Innocence"
authors: phoenix
text: |
   "Sometimes…
   Metallic butterflies and leaves,
   they measure time.
   Sometimes…
   She'd wear the brooch or the earrings
   I'd bought her,
   and I would tell her in a low voice
   how well she looked.
   Sometimes…
   The next door neighbor
   slammed his door so hard
   that the cups in the cupboard rattled.
   Sometimes…
   I'd think that on my next visit
   I should bring her a bracelet
   instead of a hair clip.
   Sometimes…
   We live the broken lives.
   Sometimes….
   We leave the broken lives behind."
