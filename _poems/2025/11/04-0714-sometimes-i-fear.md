text: |
   Sometimes I fear
   that all my theories
   are right
   except the good ones,
   that I'm only mistaken
   when I believe in their best.
   I must remember
   those who never betrayed,
   were not used as tools,
   never impersonated.
   My oldest and dearest friends?
   For a while now
   I think my nephew sold me out
   because of stupidity and money.
   My sister did so
   a long time ago,
   out of fear.
   He, though -
   only recently.
   Every loss causes pain,
   a deep cut.
   Like I'm being robbed
   of all relations,
   and of possibility to form new ones,
   of a hope for trust.
   This I do attribute to the usual suspects,
   it's exactly their MO.
   On a plus side,
   my mother's burial
   is just one more
   point on the line.
   There must be a threshold,
   a cut out,
   a limit
   for how much one can feel.
   Beyond it,
   it shouldn't be cumulative.
   Perhaps, this is the plan:
   to bring me
   to this limit gradually
   and then reveal
   the most painful loss;
   indeed,
   I might not feel it at all.
   Only execution is more important than intent:
   they so do lack finesse.
   Нельзя поручать нейрохирургию мясникам…
   And anyway,
   what if I'm an exception again
   and don't have a threshold for pain?
   What if no amount of time
   can change
   how deep it will cut,
   how much it will hurt?
   Common wisdom
   is only applicable to commons.
   Or normals -
   however you want to call it.
   It's not a presumption
   but an observation.
   Speaking of which… Every morning
   I wake up in fear these days.
   I don't think this will end well.
