text: |
   I am an overthinker.
   Which implies that I can think.
   Which is better than the opposite
   on any day of the week.
   No, not every scrap of meat
   is the best. Yes,
   with a skillful use of words,
   criteria arbitrary selective,
   even the worst may seem alright
   from some perspective
   but a clear-eyed child
   is able to pierce
   this trickery.
   Some things
   are
   better than others.
   Just like some people are simply better:
   kinder, smarter, braver,
   fairer, closer to angels.
   When a butcher hands me
   a piece of old, smelly meat
   and calls it the best,
   I call him a crook and a cheat
   and demand
   *The* best one.
