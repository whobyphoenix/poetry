---
poem_title: "The Sea Devils."
authors: phoenix
text: |
   The 3rd Doctor is too kinetic for my taste:
   all that karate, use of weapons,
   patronizing women,
   racism to aliens…
   I prefer his later incarnations
   that talk fast and get things done
   with lots of jabbering, running,
   and inspiring people
   by being never cruel,
   and never cowardly,
   and never giving up,
   and most certainly
   never giving in.
   And all that nonsense
   about an idiot-politician
   ordering brave but loyal soldiers
   to attack reptiloids torpedoing
   a beginning of a peaceful solution?
   then flipping over at a prospect
   of a praise for peacemaking
   out of vanity?
   then going
   for the nuclear option?
   Nah… Too unrealistic.
   Could've never happened.
   Although,
   the designer of this episode
   has a very fine name.. My favorite ❤️.
   And yeah:
   "reverse the polarity of the neutron flow"
   and solving the prisoners dilemma
   is totally the golden hammer.
   Hovercrafts are cool though.
