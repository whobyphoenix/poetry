text: |
   Why did you choose to hurt me?
   what could possibly be
   the other option
   if this is the right thing?
   I sometimes hurt you with words,
   only it's never deliberate, premeditated.
   It's not like I've been writing a speech
   and rehearsing it for days.
   And I am sorry by the way:
   premeditated or not,
   I shouldn't do it.
   And you -
   what could it possibly be -
   the greater evil
   that you've averted?
   When we are together
   will you keep doing it?
   The answer is Yes.
   Obviously.
   It is worth it, isn't it?
   Can you possibly understand
   a fear of happiness?
   Maybe that's why
   I was always craving a brother,
   a protector:
   could a promise
   of a punch in a face
   for every one of such times
   be a deterrent?
   I am too fragile.
   It's neither gold nor diamond
   but a crystal or glass
   already broken so many times,
   re-melted by fire,
   re-molded into something
   to be broken again.
   Can you possibly understand?
