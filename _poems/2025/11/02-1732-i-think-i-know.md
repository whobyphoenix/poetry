text: |
   I think I know what happened…
   When they gave you that apple
   and made you choose
   the right goddess,
   instead of showing patience
   and listening to all three offers
   you immediately agreed to the first -
   that of power, my beloved Paris.
   Probably, out of best intentions:
   like a chance to bring peace.
   If not Peace on Earth
   then at least in the East.
   How is it,
   that you're unaware
   of the danger
   of premature things?..
   Make offerings to Venus!
   What if she forgives?
