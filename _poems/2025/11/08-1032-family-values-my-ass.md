---
poem_title: "Family values my ass…"
authors: phoenix
text: |
   They probably told you things
   like "she's obviously
   a moneygrabber and a flimsy,
   she'll dump you for a richer guy in a year,
   or she's into power,
   or she'll cheat on you all the time,
   or, more truthfully,
   this could be a scandal
   to affect everyone you love".
   But in truth, deep down,
   they all knew it's all about
   the towers and walls,
   divides and schisms
   that must never be bridged -
   the proper order of things,
   and you're an overly imaginative
   dreamer who'll soon get over it.
---
