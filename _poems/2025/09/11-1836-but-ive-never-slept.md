text: |
   But I've never slept with Mars!
   Why would Venus
   be as cruel to me as to Eos??
   My fingers are not that rosy.
   And I don't even like horses.
   And when I said
   that I want to spend
   my old age on Mars,
   I meant the planet.
   This is a case of mistaken identity,
   a misunderstanding!
   Perhaps, due to my choosing a similar name?
   Phoenix/Sunrise/Aurora/Eos
   could be all the same to the goddess…
   Her jealous rage is misplaced:
   the God of War is not in my books
   for obvious reasons,
   nor is he interested.
   This is so unfair.
   I should file a complaint with Jupiter…
   The proper way to resolve this
   would be to lift her curse,
   of course.
   Or… alternatively…
   to let me abduct whoever I choose.
   Once.
   Which would be you, my beloved.
