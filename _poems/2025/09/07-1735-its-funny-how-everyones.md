text: |
   It's funny how everyone's
   just living their lives,
   you included, and I am stuck
   without any resolution,
   without a possibility of a resolution.
   It feels like those who were
   even remotely involved
   would like nothing more
   than my forgetting everything,
   getting back to "normal",
   whatever it is,
   and most of all:
   never ever bothering anyone ever again.
   How am I to withstand
   this collective push toward nothingness,
   the void, the dark abyss?
   They all seem to have their own ideas
   of what should be "normal" for me.
   The common ingredients,
   points of convergence
   seem to be emptiness,
   absence of happiness,
   and, of course, ignorance
   of any part of the truth.
   I do like helping the good.
   Those who assumed
   malicious reasons
   are wrong, I simply like it. 
   No strings attached.
   It's not about expecting reciprocity at all.
   I enjoy feeling helpful.
   Oh well.
   It was all predetermined
   by the silly accidents of birth,
   by your ivory towers,
   impenetrable walls.
   Nothing changes in this world.
   This story feels so old.
   It's ridiculous that I thought…
   oh, never mind, I was obviously wrong.
   It's just I do not like it
   when liars are winning.
   And they've damaged everything.
