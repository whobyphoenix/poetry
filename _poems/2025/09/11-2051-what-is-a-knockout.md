   "What is a knockout like you
   doing in a gin joint like this?"
   And then she turns.
   But something is a miss.
   Nope, Number One, it's not the same.
   She's only a facsimile.
   A knock-off.
   A mundane imitation.
   Try any variation
   you can think of,
   the saultry
   who listened so attentively
   is not to be replaced.
