text: |
   You're content,
   I get it.
   I'm not.
   And yet,
   I can never cause you harm,
   cannot disrespect.
   Do you see my dilemma?
   Yes, I know:
   my constant self-torture
   had grown old months ago.
   I am quite stuck,
   just like that needle from a haystack,
   now on the record.
   Such an antipode
   of an April wind:
   stable like that ground under your feet -
   consistent in loving you,
   consistent in my self-defeat,
   consistent in hating liars,
   in having prophetic dreams,
   in moving and never arriving.
   Consistent in seeking the truth
   and hiding from it,
   trembling with fear
   while talking of pride.
   A coward and an aspiring hero,
   both at the same time,
   consistent in my inconsistency
   and in having a temerity
   to make eyes.
   To you, no one else.
   Always.
