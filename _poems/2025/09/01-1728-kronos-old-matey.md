text: |
   Kronos, old matey,
   let's just find common enemies.
   I'm in a mood to insult deities…
   For example, Hera -
   what an overambitious old bitch!
   let's agree on this.
   And it's not *all*
   due to her spouse's roving eye
   and other roving bits.
   She's not so much a cunt as a cant -
   in the old sense:
   cant political,
   cant poetical,
   cant moral,
   cant sexual.
   Right, sorry, touchy subject again.
   And the elderly harpy
   is a terrible mother, too.
   Just ask Hephaestus,
   the honest, hardworking, albeit lame runt
   she so hurriedly discarded as an infant.
   And who, pray tell, sits on a chair
   delivered from who knows where
   just because it's shiny?
   Her stupidity is not even funny.
   Does she not know
   the story of the Trojan horse?
   Oh, right,
   it's only you and I
   who see events in that order…
   Still, some measure of clairvoyance
   for the true goddess
   is only necessary.
   And that ceremony…
   "Familiar honor" my ass!
   Utter shallowness
   and pretense at propriety.
   Not that it helped her much, mind you.
   C'mon, surely, there're more
   reasons to trash her.
   Join in!
   Remember old grievances, Kronos.
   Let the Parcae, Moirai, and Norns
   spin,
   let Moros bring
   her the lot she deserves.
