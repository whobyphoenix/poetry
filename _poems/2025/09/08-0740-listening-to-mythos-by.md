---
poem_title: "Listening to \"Mythos\" by Fry"
authors: phoenix
images: 08-0740-1.jpg
text: |
   The story of an icy moon, or a cow 
   caught in between
   two beings
   of the upper echelon,
   or a trophy, or a miracle to behold
   moves along:
   the hero is following the Guide's tail
   that wags and sways
   or a cruel hoax sent to perplex -
   we don't know yet.
   The joker's joke strays astray
   and lights keep blinking in dismay
   in Tesla's airport.
