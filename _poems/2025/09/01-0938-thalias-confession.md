---
poem_title: "Thalia's confession"
authors: phoenix
text: |
   It totally escaped my memory
   that Kronos was quite literally gelded,
   now I feel so bad
   for making fun of the disabled,
   calling him an impotent…
   I guess, I do deserve
   a divine curse for this, somewhat…
   Yet, I am tempted
   to blame it
   on mother Mnemosyne…
   We have a strange relationship with him, indeed:
   Time serving punishment before
   my transgression has even occurred.
   Perhaps, I should apologize to the poor sod.
   When something comes to mind,
   I'll write a less offensive funny rhymes.
   Who knows, maybe
   the emasculated ex-usurper
   can forgive me.
