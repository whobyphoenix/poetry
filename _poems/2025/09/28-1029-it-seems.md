text: |
   It seems
   your buddy Zeus
   is sending yet another heavy rain his way,
   just in case.
   Must be nice
   having all the heavens
   to look after your interests…
   I'm back to my assumption
   that you're Ganymede's reincarnation.
   Please make them avoid
   throwing rocks on the road,
   innocents might get hurt.
   And unlike some people
   I'm quite capable
   of saying clear,
   unambiguous, unequivocal "No"
   to his face, thank you very much.
   Divine intervention is not required as such.
   However,
   it's nice to know that you care
   enough to spoil the weather.
