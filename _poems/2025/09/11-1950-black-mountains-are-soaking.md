text: |
   Black mountains are soaking with rain
   while a blind man is teaching how to paint
   and the world is drowning in pain
   and madness.
   The light is refracted into rainbow
   by the screen I've never switched on.
   In the mirror Love looks so alone.
   And the plastic flowers
   remind me of my mother.
   Yet, even now
   what's on my mind
   is you, my beloved.
   However far you are,
   I wish you near.
