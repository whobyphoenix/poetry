---
poem_title: "Что же мне делать-то с тобой?"
authors: phoenix
text: |
   Я больше почти не паникую,
   не испытываю жуткой боли -
   так иногда прорывается только.
   Я тебя очень люблю,
   но ситуацию эту нахожу
   всё более комичной.
   Farcical somewhat.
   I think I'm at the precipice of turning a "Joker",
   not of a criminal variety, just a trickster.
   This is all too ridiculous:
   the premise and the current state of affairs.
   Comical really.
   Let's make as many random moves as possible
   while staying true to ourselves.
   Just to piss them off,
   to make their heads spin,
   to force them
   to waste as much energy as possible
   on trying to contain,
   to hide the truth.
   Let's call down all the fire upon ourselves.
   Maybe less will be left
   on doing this to others.
   I so do hate cynics.
   I guess my instrumental goal
   is to cause them as much trouble
   as I can. We'll see how it goes.
   It's nice to have nothing to lose.
   One day we'll know the truth.
---
