   if you haven't already
   for distracting you at the crucial point

   if it is not known,
   if there are too many lies,
   and people are too ignorant
   to spot even the most obvious faults in logic.
   New age requires change.
   Maybe if not for me
   you could've done things differently,
   invested more in battles of infowar.
   I'm very sorry if this is so.
   Still love you though.
