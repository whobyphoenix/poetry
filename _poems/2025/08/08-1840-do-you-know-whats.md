text: |
   Do you know what's holding me back?
   It's so silly:
   it's this ridiculous conspiracy theory
   that maybe
   I'm put through emotional swings
   for one reason only:
   to drive me to expressing dark thoughts more or less clearly.
   Then discover my body
   fallen of a bridge somewhere.
   Then leak some of the facts
   to those who
   mistakenly call themselves journalists:
   they'd publish it all in a jiffy,
   no factchecking,
   no seeking
   for the whole truth,
   the complete picture:
   it's too juicy,
   too many views and clicks,
   and if not them
   then somebody else will.
   The usual excuses.
   All to drive you to self-blame,
   making you hurt.
   And this ridiculous theory
   with near-zero probability
   is my thought of last resort
   and the reason why
   I avoid bridges, towers,
   and staying in rooms on high floors.
   I don't care how crazy this sounds:
   I refuse to be used against you any more.
