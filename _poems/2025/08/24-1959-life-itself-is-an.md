---
poem_title: "\"Life itself is an exercise in exceptions.\""
authors: phoenix
text: |
   A new carbon molecule
   is an exact copy,
   except when on occasion
   it happens, a mutation:
   often a horrid mistake,
   but sometimes - a miracle.
   Like when you meet all these people,
   see faces, souls, eyes,
   and it's just a repetition
   of the same routine motion,
   except when on occasion
   it happens, you fall in love:
   often a horrid mistake
   but sometimes - a miracle.
   With you though it feels like fate.
   Like it was exactly
   how it's supposed to be,
   couldn't have happened any differently.
   I wish to tell you all this to your face
   one day.
   Or not:
   I'd probably be very awkward
   and silent as a fish or a cactus.
   Just in case
   you plan to do this too -
   pretending to be a cactus -
   you need a backup plan,
   something with no words required.
   My backup plan is a hug as always.
   And just in case,
   may I also suggest
   you have a backup for a backup?
   It's only prudent with our track record…
   That old idea
   about having us locked somewhere
   for a long enough time?
   it's not a bad one…
   A week should do it,
   but better make it a month
   to be on a safe side.
   Or simply throw out the keys,
   and everyone will be happy.
