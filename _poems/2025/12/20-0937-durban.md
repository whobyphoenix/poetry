poem_title: "Durban"
authors: phoenix
images: 20-0937-1.jpg, 20-0937-2.jpg
text: |
   The more I'm here
   the more it is clear
   that South Africa is indeed divided
   into "us" and "them".
   They better fix it,
   it's not healthy at all:
   can't be a basement for the bright future
   in any sense of the word.
   Maybe try subsidizing flights
   for East-European tourists,
   the more the merrier?
   It should in time
   dissolve the barriers
   that locals can't overcome,
   not by themselves,
   not without external help.
   And it helps when an outsider
   just doesn't see them:
   your imaginary fences
   and color variations -
   you all are South Africans,
   all are equals to foreigners.
