text: |
   What property has the alphabet,
   that
   it can paint
   the white egret
   standing there,
   in the uncurled leaves
   of the lilies?
   And when she's silent, she's still a song.
   And when she's still, she's flickering:
   a white flutter in the sunbeam.
   For a moment I believed
   that the light came from her,
   that the forest had invented
   a new way of shining.
   Then she lifted her wings
   and softly glided over
   the dark water,
   to soar free
   above the trees.
