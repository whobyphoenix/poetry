---
poem_title: "Performance"
authors: phoenix
text: |
   Like a tiny acrobat
   performing a dance
   with a ball on a string,
   so close to the circus ceiling;
   or a priest with a censer
   performing a service - a bird,
   smaller than a sparrow, is cleaning
   his beak:
   he mistook
   a dry speck
   that was caught
   in a spiderweb
   for an insect
   and now is all entangled
   in the consequences of the act.

   (True story. И лучше бы этот птиц ловил комаров, чтобы меня меньше ели. Они тут страшные звери.)
