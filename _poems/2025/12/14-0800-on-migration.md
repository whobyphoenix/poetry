---
poem_title: "On migration."
authors: phoenix
text: |
   The wind is a result
   of pressure differences.
   The temporary solution is
   to close all doors and windows.
   But what if
   the wind
   becomes too strong
   to hold it off?
   After all,
   its strength is influenced
   by the pressure gradient.
   Another way to solve it
   is,
   of course,
   to equalize the pressure somewhat
   by either degrading living standards
   in your own place
   by, say, dissolving checks and balances,
   suppressing freedoms -
   surely, a no-no…
   Or - helping them improve things
   in where they come from.
---
