---
poem_title: "Pegasus Begins"
authors: phoenix
text: |
   Jupiter's minister of nautical affairs
   once loved a beautiful maiden
   which isn't a crime, who cares.
   Only the naughty did
   the naughty deed
   in a temple (!).
   Now *this* necessitated a curse.
   On the woman of course.
   Thus came to being the winged horse.
---
