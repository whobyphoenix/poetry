text: |
   Or is this you, my beloved,
   giving me something to fight?
   I am unable to fly:
   the air is too empty,
   too unresisting,
   unstruggling.
   Water, however,
   совсем другое дело:
   all this substance to navigate,
   waves upon waves upon waves…
   "It's the status quo." is not
   the same as "No.".
   I'm sorry but this is so.
   Be more specific next time.
   Meanwhile, good luck living a lie.
   C'mon, you can do better.
   You know exactly what to do.
   Where's the other shoe?
