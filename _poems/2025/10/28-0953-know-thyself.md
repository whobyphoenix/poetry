---
poem_title: "Know thyself"
authors: phoenix
text: |
   According to my own model
   of a human soul;
   this is I who's "in between
   forsaken and foreseen".
   I most certainly can never find a beast in me:
   blood is too meaningless,
   all children are children -
   my loyalty is to principles
   not families or pack-leaders.
   Nor can I find a demon:
   my love is to complex souls
   not inanimate ideas.
   I see trees in forests
   and people in nations,
   try to avoid generalizations,
   and am too keen
   on self-sacrifice for a demon.
   I can never be only a human;
   I just can't; don't ask why.
   While I'm sensing your love
   I am a heavenly creature:
   this is
   only too obvious.
   The rest of the time
   I'm a forever hungry hero.
   Envisioning *more* for others, a dreamer.
   I never knew I have a want,
   it was dormant -
   thanks to wrong or misunderstood teachings.
   Cohen has awoken this in me,
   he must've been a kin,
   or perhaps - a true mirror.
   And now I know
   the hunger.
   This is like taking a bite of an apple 
   and losing shores on one's eyes,
   awakening,
   losing ability to stay content in lies.
   To hell with "paradise":
   content is lost and good riddance.
   So.
   Not only am I
   multi-dimensional,
   but a combination
   of *rare* colors…
   This makes me almost unique
   and perhaps dangerous.
   Is this why?
