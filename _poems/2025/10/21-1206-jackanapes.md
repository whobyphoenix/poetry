poem_title: "Jackanapes"
authors: phoenix
images: 21-1206-1.jpg, 21-1206-2.jpg, 21-1206-3.jpg, 21-1206-4.jpg
text: |
   I admit.
   I am a pompous, self-opinionated idiot.
   And you are so infernally touchy.
   Yes... Not a single dull day...
   A very promising way
   to spend an eternity,
   don't you think, honey?
