text: |
   I pace the grounds
   awaiting your arrival
   while plagues and wars
   continue to claim
   thousands lives a day.
   I confess that I'm stuck
   unable to solve the riddle
   without true data, new input,
   without fresh facts.
   My brain just churns
   round and round
   like a wheel stuck in mud
   as I pace the grounds.
   Do you wish to face
   the judges of the afterworld
   with an unclean soul,
   my false immodest
   and unconjoined?
   The thing they detest
   is unfinished quests.
   Not sure how to weave-in
   the metaphor of the bulging mouth of the wineskin
   you are not to loosen…
   better end with the question then.
   So, when?
