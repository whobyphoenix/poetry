---
poem_title: "Listening to \"Troy\" by Fry"
authors: phoenix
text: |
   So, that's why
   gods-worshiping-cultures
   generally fared better:
   smart and wise advisors
   were able to hide
    their cunning and intelligence
   from cruel and vain
   by pretending they're "guided",
   "enthused", "inspired"-
   mere vessels
   for the divine council
   or prophecies.
   If not for this vail,
   they'd surely be slaughtered out of envy,
   their murderers self-robbed of their wisdom,
   left to listen to flattery and blind obedience…
   And of course,
   this "gods work in mysterious ways"
   line to explain away
   an occasional failure in judgement,
   something not pinning out
   due to unforeseen challenges…
   All-in-all, brilliant survival strategy!
   Would they still choose you as the scapegoat
   if you pretended to be a prophet,
   beloved?
   Yes, I do
   think shamanic attire would look nice on you..
---
