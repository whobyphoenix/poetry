---
poem_title: "Pixilated."
authors: phoenix
books: las-agujas
text: |
   Your craic and geg October gag
   turned out scundered,
   the message - scarpered.
   Although, I liked the outfit:
   I dream of burning it.
   And your topknot -
   to comb or to cut -
   can't decide.
   And of tackling you - a bit -
   with my "girlish ardor".
   At the crepuscular hour.
   All that sham nonchalance!
   It really took the biscuit.
   And I decry
   that lugubrious smile.
   As every eye in the sky knows:
   I am strictly for the birds,
   and you can't get blood
   out of that turnip of yours.
   Or do I cast aspersions,
   your joke-not-excellence?
   Phantom, horrible asseverations...
---
