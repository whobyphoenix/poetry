text: |
   Maybe love has mechanics
   of an autoimmune disease:
   you occupy my thoughts,
   your constant presence
   causes inflammation.
   But you are unrepellable,
   and I am unmendable.
   Mr. Persistency…
   Don't even have a decency
   to age properly:
   how are you getting sexier, you brute?
   How rude…
   You possess my thoughts.
   Inflammation grows.
   Temperature rises causing damage -
   until one of the two happens.
   Only I am too weird
   to end up like regular people,
   so, with me it's the third option:
   you turn into a chronic condition.
   FYI, don't want to be cured
   but do require remedies for the symptoms.
   Something like that "distilled Carduus Benedictus":
   to "live in thy heart, die in thy lap, and be buried in thy eyes."
   Shakespeare and I: we know a thing or two about love.
