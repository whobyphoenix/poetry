text: |
   I don't understand your world at all,
   it is completely alien:
   all this obsession with papers and words -
   why would I care?
   And yet somehow they all do.
   And it's not like they don't break rules -
   they twist the weights:
   important stuff (like lives and love)
   becomes meaningless
   and they claim to suffer because of pronouns.
   Do they all live double lives
   harboring alter egos,
   or is there truly nothing there -
   beyond this facade: a shallow
   shell with a shiny smile
   uttering nothing but cheerful chitchat,
   nonsense void of meaning?
   But it can't be all,
   it's too unnatural for a human being
   to be this doll -
   always lying through their teeth
   about how great life is,
   never saying anything of weight
   like it's impolite to think deeply.
   Like depths of the soul
   is yet another genitalia -
   never to be displayed or explored
   in polite company, never in public.
   And when discourse
   is nearing the Truth
   they tend to joke it off
   restoring the proper levity,
   the amoebic propriety;
   to look away and never in -
   like the scariest thing
   is the boogeyman within.
   Are they all children?
