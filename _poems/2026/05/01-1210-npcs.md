---
poem_title: "NPCs"
authors: phoenix
text: |
   They claim to like diversity,
   they looove minorities,
   yet are incapable of accepting existence
   of those who are truly one of a kind.
   It is as if the very idea of ​​uniqueness
   were an affront to them, a personal insult:
   an enemy to break, to force-conform,
   or to reject and attack on every turn.
---
