text: |
   It hangs by a thread, a hair thin.
   It flickers - the leftover warmth within.
   It breathes out, to breathe in.
   The ask is a must.
   The mast is unjust.
   The dancer is never the one to trust.
   Oxidation.
   Trepidation.
   A weightless sensation.
   Dare or glances?
   Truth or consequences?
   And there goes the flames.
