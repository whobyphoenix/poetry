---
poem_title: "Do you have ears?"
authors: phoenix
text: |
   You asked about the place of Life.
   Or was it Light? I couldn't hear.
   My ears are on fire.
   It's lanterns, stars
   and birds of light
   that make it bright.
   And darkness wins
   when they don't shine.
   And there is darkness isn't there?
   Or is it that annoying beam
   stuck in the apple of my eye
   that makes me blind?
   Or is it light that's beaming out?
   So what was it you asked about?
