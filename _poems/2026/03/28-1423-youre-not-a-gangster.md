authors: phoenix
text: |
   You're not a gangster,
   never wanted to be a bad guy.
   You're just a loner, right?
   Deep down on the inside?
   And I've gotten in your way,
   still don't know exactly how or why.
   It's not easy having yourself a good time,
   greasing up those bets and betters.
   I can never understand this,
   but for you
   it may be the only way to get by.

   And so, now you can't decide
   whether I should live or die.
   Yes, I'll probably go to heaven,
   shouldn't hang my head and cry.
   It's no wonder
   why
   your heart feels dead inside -
   so cold, and hard, and petrified:
   they've brought you back into the fold
   with so much bullying that now
   you don't dare to even peek outside.

   It's a bitch convincing people to like you.
   One must go a fullblown conformist,
   sing only in chorus,
   choose only mediocrity.

   If you were to stop they'd call you disloyal.
   And for you it's so important what neighbors think.
   That's why you dance that jigs,
   making the moves so well rehearsed, so ready to please.

   I've made you angry,
   made you uncomfortable:
   I'm the truth devotee,
   couldn't care any less what that neighbors think.
   But yeah, you're right:
   having me around is like walking with a shit-magnet.
   If lies were cats I'd be a litter.
   Pleasing everyone isn't for me.

   You could throw me in the lake
   or feed me poisoned birthday cake.
   I know you'll miss me when I'm gone.
   Or you could bury me alive
   but I might crawl out with a knife
   and kill you when you're sleeping, and that's exactly why
   you can't decide
   whether I should live or die.
   Yes, I'll probably go to heaven,
   shouldn't hang my head and cry.
   So, are you taking me for a ride?
   Should I lock the doors and close the blinds?
   Or am I something you've forgotten -
   curled up dying, getting rotten -
   without a chance to learn why?
