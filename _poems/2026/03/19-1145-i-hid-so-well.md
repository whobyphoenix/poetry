authors: phoenix
text: |
   I hid so well,
   until you saw me,
   recognized who I am.
   That revelation cost me dearly.
   Now Old Nick's minions chase me -
   always - getting closer and closer;
   trying to become fleas on my tail:
   good thing I'm tailless.
   New acquaintances
   are never my confidantes.
   Only ancient friends.
   Of all humanity,
   I share this world with about a dozen twins,
   and yet I'm too trusting:
   the circle of evil is narrowing.
   They're growing bolder, more daring:
   good thing I'm strong-headed.
   I listen to ancient oldies,
   and still find them golden.
   Today it's Duke and Johnny.
   I still begin my mornings
   with tender, sweet visions
   of your smile and your eyes, honey.
   Now who is Consistency?
