authors: phoenix

text: |
   It's not pining! It's passed on! This regime is no more! It has ceased to be! It is expired and gone to meet its maker! It's a stiff! Bereft of life, it rests in peace! It's pushing up the daisies! Its metabolic processes are now history! It's off the twig! It's kicked the bucket,
   it's shuffled off its mortal coil, run down the curtain and joined the bleeding choir invisible! This is an ex-theocracy!
   —
   Yes, IRGC junta (panicking old men with death breathing heavily down their shoulders, hiding in bunkers, probably not slept for about a month) are "negotiating".
   But they know much better than outsiders that they can't possibly deliver on any long term promises: that's why they will promise anything, only what they really want is assurances of personal safety.
   They are basically terrorists holding hostages: psychologists are required, not diplomats…
