text: |
   He consistently makes
   the same mistake in his analysis:
   doesn't take people into account.
   Most of the times
   this simplification is ok
   but in the real crisis
   it's all about people - individuals -
   with their personal beliefs snd fears.
   It's like he's using Newton laws
   to compute the Mercury's orbit:
   results he's getting are way off.
   Only it happened to him before,
   he should've learned by now
   about applicability of certain models.
   It's probably because
   he never observed it up close.
   Or somehow has managed not to
   internalize the knowledge.
