---
poem_title: "Liars."
authors: phoenix
text: |
   Is any of your porcelain dolls
   a great person? Obviously not.
   Otherwise, they wouldn't need your crutches,
   you scripting their words and actions.
   They reek of insincerity,
   and no one likes it.
   That's why you're always on the lookout
   for real potential and talent.
   But the problem is that greatness
   defies classifications.
   You find them and they turn
   into a constant headache
   and major disappointment:
   tend to have opinions,
   to go off script,
   to stay completely free
   of your pack-loyalty,
   to make determinations
   with utter disregard to your authority
   and that of your "experts".
   Great is by definition nonconformist.
   But it is too inconvenient,
   and you try to make them fit
   into the same old squares
   where you keep
   your usual porcelains.
   Hence the obvious result:
   the spark is gone; you're left
   with selling dolls on crutches.
   As always.
---
