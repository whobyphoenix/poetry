   like pretending that de-escalation always means

   the lives and futures of the less brave.

   that will surely come
   soon after you reach your agreements
   with the murderers.
   Think of those you've
   marked for sacrifice
   as thousands of heroes:
   why are you so willing to abandon them
   for your chimeras?
   They are so incredibly rare.
   They are precious.
   Must you always sacrifice them?
   What does your conscience say?
   Are you blind to consequences
   because you don't care
   or is this true blindness?
