   I propose to shorten the timeframe of our bet:
   if it's still up and running
   by your birthday, then
   you win - provisionally -
   however, when it does fall
   by the end of the year,
   then I become the ultimate winner.
   Sounds fair?
   Provisional winning means any wish
   except a wish for infinite wishes;
   while the ultimate win has no restrictions -
   anything goes. Aaanything…
   Sounds reasonable?
   And it's on you to figure out
   how to make your wish -
   *if* you win provisionally;
   which I don't think can happen,
   so… it's a moot point really.
   But just in case, I suggest
   you plan for this eventuality.
