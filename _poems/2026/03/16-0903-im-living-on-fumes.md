text: |
   I'm living on fumes
   and it's never enough.
   Well hidden like a bus
   in a submarine. Like "Why Ann?"
   or T in my heart.
   Your voice makes me hot.
   Cohen's voice moves me up,
   shifts, opens the canal.
   Too ancient for this time.
   In a non existent armor.
   Too humongous for their minuscule minds
   composed of mundane thoughts, petty storylines.
   A behemoth dancing
   at the tip, the pointy end.
   Never shielded.
   Always broken.
   Never healing.
   With flesh wounds revealing
   only the void. Bleeding.
   Always bleeding.
   Like that white star.
   Like all that's Light.
   Prophets, like Cohen,
   keep the door open.
   Always welcoming.
   And here at the threshold
   I hesitate, still waiting
   for your hand.
   For I am the one.
