text: |
   Seriously, why this constant dread
   for the second day in a row?
   What has happened?
   Feels like an irreversible loss:
   like the final score has been tallied,
   the short straw drawn,
   the killing blow dealt,
   the last thread cut,
   the enemy's victory announced,
   account closed.
   Like there is nothing more.
