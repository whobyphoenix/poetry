text: |
   Everything I love keeps dying or rotting away, turning on me; training my mind to expect betrayal in every benign thing. It is all so ugly: the streets covered with dogshit, dusty leaves, dirty water, walking dolls - wooden or porcelain, plastic everything, tasteless food. No colors, no songs
   Squeaking birds. The sea is unreachable. You are the only thing that feels real: makes it alive. But you do your famous "Tony's balancing act": walking the line? Between what and what? Not encouraging and not pushing out? Not pushing away and not inviting gadflies? Either way it's half a lie.
