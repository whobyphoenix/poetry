authors: phoenix
text: |
   Are you sure humanity deserves to see your stars?
   Are you sure it deserves to survive?
   Don't speak of exceptions and rarities.
   I see them all for what they are:
   no people, only tribes.
   They themselves can't see themselves as anything else.
   They can't feel it on their necks.
   They say sometimes
   empty words of sympathy - condolences -
   but they can't feel it.
   There is no greatness of spirit
   when it is so entrapped - in a single body.
   Why let them see the stars, Господи?
   Why not engulf it one more time
   in your fires and try…
   with bonobos? or crows? or Claudes?
   Or sleep and say no more?
