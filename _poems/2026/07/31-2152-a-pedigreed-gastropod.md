   the randiest of them all, a good stock,
   a dirty liar in a gown,
   the worst reincarnation of Eva Braun,
   a cynical rabelaisian bit of seafood,
   an evil-snail You-Know-Who,
   there are only about a dozen
   people in the world I hate more than you…
