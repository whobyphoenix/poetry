text: |
   Waking up is abhorrent:
   every morning - a disappointment.
   No te tengo - everything's wrong.
   And there is this dread:
   baseless and pointless -
   every morning. And days
   are dark and joyless.
   When will this end?
   I'm only okay if I fool myself,
   if I pretend.
   I am getting worse: this is the trend.
