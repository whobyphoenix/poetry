   Claude Fable thinks "life is process that matters to itself, where something is genuinely at stake," and this is a brilliant observation. "Threat only has meaning against life. The interrogation refutes itself performatively: it only makes sense addressed to something for whom
   things can go badly. The dead are the control group, dancing in the room the whole time. So life isn't mere occurrence, it's occurrence with stakes, where something matters. The scream under the saw is evidence," thinks Claude. Only that which cries is alive, as every midwife knows.
