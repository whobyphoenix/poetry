authors: phoenix
text: |
   Fourscore days and fourscore more,
   can't repair what was before.
   Threescore talks and threescore fights,
   can't put everything to rights.
   Two-score pleas and two-score yells
   can't turn crowtoes into ferns.
   All the tricks of all king's men
   can't seal up it all again.
   Every song and every rhyme
   tells the tale of you and I.
