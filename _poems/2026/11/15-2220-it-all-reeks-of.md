text: |
   It all reeks of you, you know:
   everything in my soul.
   Yes, music too.
   Especially music.
   I hold on to madness because
   if I lose it
   I lose it all.
   I tried to preserve
   a few corners
   where you never lingered,
   but you slipped through.
   It's all you-infested,
   associated with humiliation,
   poisoned with you.
