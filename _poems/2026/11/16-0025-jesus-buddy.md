   Jesus, buddy,
   isn't it good
   that you don't love me?
   Imagine if you did
   and wanted me
   for your bride no less,
   but I kept choosing
   this man with no redeeming qualities?
   If I were you, I would so go to war,
   and then had a trial -
   to punish the guilty
   without aggrandizing him unduly
   by singling him out.
