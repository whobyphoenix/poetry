text: |
   Have this aversion
   to female faces online -
   all with her very wide smile.
   Invokes disgust.
   So unremarkable…
   Why do they all look alike:
   same hair, same features,
   same face more or less?
   How do you tell them apart?
   Do they wear badges?
   chip them like dogs
   and use that doohickey
   to tell their numbers?
