text: |
   When he opened the seal,
   I heard 'Come!' the voice says.
   And I looked, and behold a pale horse.
   And its rider's name was Death,
   and Hades were his followers.
   And power was given unto them
   over the 4th part of the earth,
   to kill.
   And amongst their weaponry
   were such elements
   as plague and pestilence.
