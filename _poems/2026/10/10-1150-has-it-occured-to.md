text: |
   Has it occured to any theologist ever
   that gods don't answer prayers
   (because gods don't exist - duh -
   but let's pretend otherwise and answer thus)
   because people are too uninteresting, too much alike,
   nearly indistinguishable from one another?
   A cacophony of voices
   saying exactly the same,
   again and again:
   same words,
   same sentences,
   same intonations…
   Picking out melodies
   from this grey noise
   would feel like torture. No entity
   would ever put themselves
   through this voluntarily.
   Not for long.
   It is unrealistic, cruel, and wrong
   to expect this of gods.
   And yet they do. All of us.
