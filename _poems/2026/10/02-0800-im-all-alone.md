   I'm all alone
   behind the wall,
   the wall is tall:
   you are the most of it
   but not all;
   it's also all the sentences
   never to confess;
   too much time has past;
   I've lost the drive to connect:
   why do it if the most important can't be said.
   Funny how
   you are not the sentence
   you are the tower now.
   How sad.
