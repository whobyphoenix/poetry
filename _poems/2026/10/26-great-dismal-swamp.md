authors: phoenix
books: sine-quem-non
text: |   
   Mr. Crêpekin,
   the princekin,
   bayou-born and bred,
   once found himself a pet —
   a century back, give or take.
   Meant just to play
   with her funny alligator pumps, not to bed...
   Except,
   couldn't quite handle that
   bayou milkshake.
   The morning revealed his mistake:
   the princeling's in a haystack
   with a murderous headache
   and a croc.
   Great buskins and socks!
   Not a moment later,
   she insisted he make her an honest alligator;
   was either that or singing soprano —
   he couldn't say no.
   So here we go:
   decades of
   abuse in the great dismal swamp,
   right under everyone's nose.
