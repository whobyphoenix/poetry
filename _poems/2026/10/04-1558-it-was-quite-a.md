   It was quite a blunder
   to place me in a Prisoner's Dilemma:
   I can never defect -
   it's too dishonorable.
   I was raised
   on the wisdom and with a mindset
   of Soviet dissidents:
   I never trust jailers.
   I have zero doubt:
   they always lie,
   No matter if the other one defects.
   I won't, that's all that matters.
