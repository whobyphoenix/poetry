text: |
   Let me tell you how it will be:
   whatever it is
   that you're avoiding,
   your worst nightmare that's making you afraid -
   it will happen anyway,
   as a direct consequence of steps you take
   in trying to prevent it.
   This reality of ours
   is ironic as hell:
   that's how it teaches us,
   not to be cowards.
