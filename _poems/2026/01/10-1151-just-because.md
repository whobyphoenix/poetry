---
poem_title: "Just because."
authors: phoenix
text: |
   It's tale, told by a sage,
   with noise and rage
   setting the stage
   for our avalanche;
   a day, after day, after day
   dusting off words
   from high shelves,
   forgotten chests,
   deepening wells;
   sanding off syllables
   to the very last one... Hooray!
   Out, out, brief candles!
   This is the way!
---
