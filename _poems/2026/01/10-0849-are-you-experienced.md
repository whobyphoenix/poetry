---
poem_title: "Are you experienced?"
authors: phoenix
text: |
   Hey, Jimi-baby,
   it may be a confusion, you say? Maybe...
   All the nameless colors, foghorn-louds.
   All the beats and pounds,
   swings and roundabouts.
   You touch the sun, so big and round,
   and you find out:
   starlight is raining down.
