   More interesting than a bunch of rocks?
   Are you?
   I can stare at a bunch of rocks,
   find beauty in a bunch of rocks,
   tell the truth to a bunch of rocks,
   ask questions and get silence from a bunch of rocks.
   I can never be deceived by a bunch of rocks.
   What is it you think youve got on a bunch of rocks?
