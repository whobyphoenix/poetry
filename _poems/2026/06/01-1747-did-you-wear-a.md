text: |
   Did you wear a collar under the bowtie?
   Do you put it on every morning?
   Or is it optional, reserved for public events only?
   Is your breathing that shallow?
   Is this a skill to halfbreathe
   or were you born like this?
   Same question about doublethink.
   You see, God isn't a concept, it's this light
   that separates black and white,
   wrong and right.
   When you decide
   to stay ambiguous,
   replace clarity with duplicity,
   reject what you call the "binary thinking":
   you reject this light.
   The truth is singular.
   And it does exist.
   Despite all the discomfort
   that you experience as a result of this.
