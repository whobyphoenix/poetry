text: |
   What Lennon says in his "God"
   in the first verse
   I oppose.
   I say it in reverse.
   In the second, yes,
   most assuredly.
   Except I also don't believe
   in me or reality.
   You - maybe.
   In the third, to every line,
   I declare it wrong.
   I am an insufferable contrarian,
   aren't I? Still on?
