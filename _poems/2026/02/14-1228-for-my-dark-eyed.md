text: |
   For my dark-eyed, stout, and icy-cold,
   not so young and definitely not bold.

   Tis couple of years since you look at me,
   with your fiery eyes, my muted delight…
   Your hands say "You may
   drive me out of your mind,
   another you'll surely find,
   for love turns aside
   and soon cold has grown
   like the winter's morning."
   Like the winter's morning,
   the hills are white with snow,
   and my heart is rolling,
   rolling in its alcove.
   I said I never forsake
   my love for a mistake.
   You should say
   something for heaven's sake,
   for a cloudy morning.
   For a cloudy morning
   brings in a sunny day.
