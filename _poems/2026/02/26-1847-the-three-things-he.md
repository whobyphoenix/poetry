---
poem_title: "The three things he must've said to Thomas (13):"
authors: phoenix
text: |
   1. there's too much confusion and no relief, but there's a way out of here;
   2. know yourself - yourself, don't imitate me, man;
   3. if you insist on being my mirror, they will smash that mirror to pieces with flame-throwing stones and burn themselves.
