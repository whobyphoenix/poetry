---
poem_title: "Davey"
authors: phoenix
text: |
   Well, if you don't want
   your only fan
   asking things "every night"
   then perhaps
   you should go Finn-the-human style:
   change your appearance,
   live in log cabins,
   spend days sweeping brooms.
   Only please no shave-offs:
   it'd be like destroying an art work…
   Go with sunglasses,
   you know how it works.
---
