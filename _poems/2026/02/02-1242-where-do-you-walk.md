---
poem_title: "...where do you walk these nights?"
authors: phoenix
text: |
   In the pines, in the pines,
   where the Moon does never shine;
   on the slopes
     of the Hill
       of Sorrows;
   where I shiver through and through,
   so cold without you,
   without your eyes -
   kissed off by frostbite.
   ...and what do you see these nights?
    
   All the pines, all the pines,
   needles snuffing all the light,
   shadows swallowed
     whole
       by snow,
   and I shiver through and through,
   so cold without you,
   without your eyes -
   kissed off by frostbite.
   ...and where do you go these nights?
    
   To the pines, to the pines,
   where the Moon does never shine;
   where the cold wind blows,
   where no one ever goes;
   where I shiver through and through,
   so cold without you,
   seeing your eyes
   every time I close mine.
