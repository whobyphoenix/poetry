text: |
   Yes, baby, all that's left
   is to lay down your head
   and weep for them
   in this drunkyard,
   where only the two of us
   see them as sleepwalkers;
   the two afflicted with the same;
   and frankly, the prettiest.
   We should form a pack
   or just share a glance
   in the soberness of the fact
   that there's no one else.
