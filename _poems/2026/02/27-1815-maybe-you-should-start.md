text: |
   Maybe you should start thinking
   about your stage pseudonym
   for that time when you're a refugee
   from that dystopia that's unfolding…
   Mr. Consistence-and-Clarity is a bit of a mouthful…
   How about Silver Fox?
   Two-Wars-Guy?
   Dino? Baby Blue?
   Phoenix's Lover?
