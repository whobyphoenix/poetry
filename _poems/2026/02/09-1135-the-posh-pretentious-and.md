text: |
   The posh, pretentious and vain,
   hiding their sins, their pasts,
   wearing masks,
   smiling on commands -
   they instill so much distrust.
   The way they talk, behave -
   it's all alien.
   Porcelain.
   Hiding so much of themselves…
   It's only too obvious
   that they fear us -
   most of humanity.
   They must have reasons.
