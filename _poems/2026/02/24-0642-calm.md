poem_title: "Calm?"
authors: phoenix

text: |
   A rep for conviction, hey? You and the Arrow…
   "Calm under pressure"
   simply begs for clarification
   like how calm under how much pressure,
   calmness measured how, etc.
   Why are people never specific? Right…
   It's because Mr Clarity can't be bothered to write
   for his own website,
   thus all the unclarity… Kudos
   to that team member of yours
   who thought up the three words
   starting with C.
   (Stephen Fry-style or OCD?)
   I'm just teasing:
   of course all the brilliant ideas are yours
   'cause you are not only a cutie,
   but also smart, right sweetie?
   I merely want you in my bed -
   every night. How is this a big ask?
   Meanwhile, this street-cat
   sleeps in my bed:
   she's finally succumbed
   to my cat-charms
   and allows me to pet
   her, but only in the dark.
