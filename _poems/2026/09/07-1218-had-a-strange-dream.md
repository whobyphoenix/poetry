text: |
   Had a strange dream
   heavy on symbolism.
   Honey, how many
   heads do you have lately?
   The thing is, in the dream
   there was this dog, you see -
   two headed dog on a screen.
   The second one kind of hanging
   on a thick neck
   on the dog's back.
   One understands full well
   one's obvious tells.
   One prefers clarity.
