text: |
   I am a gift.
   The best there can be.
   And he knows it.
   Yet he dismisses me,
   misunderstands his duty:
   to be the best he can -
   especially around them;
   to live the real life -
   without cowardice or lies;
   to show that love exists -
   the true promise.
   Between the only two high ways
   he chooses… to wait?
