---
poem_title: "Однажды на болоте:"
authors: phoenix
images: 18-1048-1.jpg, 18-1048-2.jpg
text: |
   "Did you know that U does not only pay us but also provides taxi services? I recently tried and it's an interesting experience: exotic, peculiar, invigorating. Because there is this driver, you see, and no one vetted him, and you can't be certain in advance of anything.
   A bit like a casino, I suppose, or hiring an escort without looking at pictures first. A big part of their appeal, I think, is this randomness involved. In any case, I tried and I survived. I admit, I was nervous, sent shivers down my spine: who knows what might have happened. An exotic experience."
