   The distance between us
   could not be greater,
   the divide - more impassable,
   or the chasm - wider.
   Generations,
   genetic completeness
   resulting in all the differences
   in brain chemistry,
   upbringing,
   education, experiences,
   what places
   in life and on the planet
   are available to us.
   Different universes…
