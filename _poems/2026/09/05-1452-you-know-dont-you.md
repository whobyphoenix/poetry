   You know, don't you,
   why I christened you Baby Blue?
   It was that one time,
   you were quoting a black guy,
   an ancient bluesman:
   and you said "I'm blue",
   and it was so You.
   And you looked so deliciously cute,
   that it turned into Baby Blue.
   That's how it happened.
