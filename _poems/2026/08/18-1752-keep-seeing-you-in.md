   Keep seeing you in my dreams lately.
   Almost nightly.
   (Much prefer this
   to my usual nightmares

   of a rather low opinion of you, baby:

   you are absent.
   Why is that?
   Any ideas?
   My dreams paint you as unreliable,
   yet, very appealing.
