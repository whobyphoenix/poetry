text: |
   If you ever need
   a sigil for a seal
   or a coat of arms
   or a heraldic badge
   for all those waving flags
   on your castle battlements
   then I recommend a toucan.
   Vue de profil
   obviously.
   Toucans:
   proud possessors
   of prominent prowess.
   The sigil would mean
   a "form of excellency
   with nothing former about it"
