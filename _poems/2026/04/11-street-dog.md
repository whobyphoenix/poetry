poem_title: "Tramp's song."
authors: phoenix
books: las-agujas
text: |
   I’m not a “good stock”.
   I am a street dog.
   My pedigree
   is not to be.
   Hey, baby,
   what’s your name?
   Lady?
   Now let me see
   what’s under your tail
   and how well you shake it.
   Hey, let’s fuck in a bush!
   What? too lowbrow for you?
   Not your uzh?
   What chip? Are you nuts?
   My number is known only to gods.
   Or not.
   When my breed goes
   to Dogs’ Heaven
   they decline the entrance:
   we dig tunnels
   under their fences.
   This is my stance:
   no chips, no passports, no stamps.
   Why are you meowing like a cat,
   all these long words in triplets?
   I don’t know what they mean,
   go buy me a dictionary.
   My vocabulary
   is in short barks only.
   But my tail is long
   and my dick is hard:
   come love me, baby!
   Aren’t you a lady?
   I am a street dog.
