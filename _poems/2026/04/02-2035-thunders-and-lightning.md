text: |
   Thunders and lightning…
   Rain feels the same:
   Northern Hemisphere, Southern…
   This world is so small
   and so packed with border controls.
   I wanted to see it all -
   human majesty, auroras.
   But it's for others.
   It's strange
   how speed of travel is increased,
   and yet a medieval pilgrimage could reach
   what I am not allowed.
   How funny…
   Ancient cities are as inaccessible as Space to me.
   This mode of being -
   fleeing with the wind
   or simply fleeing -
   does take its tall.
   Struck by the Fall,
   or simply struck, not running, no:
   I'd never run.
   It's homelessness, not seeking.
   When will you catch me?
